Connection modes: Run node server.js on the teacher's laptop and open http://localhost:3000 there. Students join from their phones using the address shown on the teacher screen. It works over a phone hotspot, laptop hotspot or school Wi-Fi/router — no internet needed. If the green LAN badge doesn't show, you're in demo mode (same device, separate tabs only).
